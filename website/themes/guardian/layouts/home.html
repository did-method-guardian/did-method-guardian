Group guardianship

Any identity may be overseen by one or more guardians -- transferable, terminable, governed by configurable consensus thresholds. No other DID method has this as a first-class relationship.

Auditable and verifiable

All guardian recovery actions are signed by the guardian's key, which stay attributable in the replayed log.

Built on did:plc and did:web

did:plc

  • proven operation log
  • signing scheme
  • recovery-window mechanism

+ did:web

  • human readable accessibility
  • multi-authority resilience

= did:guardian

  • + guardians, as a first-class relationship
