Group guardianship

Any identity may be overseen by one or more guardians -- transferable, terminable, governed by configurable consensus thresholds. No other DID method has this as a first-class relationship.

No impersonation, ever

A guardian's recovery action is always signed by the guardian's own, distinct key -- never the ward's. Every action stays attributable in the replayed log.

Built on did:plc

Directly adopts did:plc's proven operation log, signing scheme, and recovery-window mechanism -- extended, not reinvented, where this method genuinely differs.
